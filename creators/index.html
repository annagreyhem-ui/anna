About Anna

Hi, I'm Anna. At 29 I had achieved what I had dreamed of. I became a psychologist and a business consultant. But somehow I wasn't happy. So I quit and felt like a failure.

Now I chase dreams and white rabbits. I write about longing, love and death, the things that live at the edges. How Life Feels… is my first book, and the place where all of it begins.
