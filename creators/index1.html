How Life Feels…

How Life Feels... weaves together poetry, short stories, and prose into a search for the self. Along the way, it explores losing yourself, falling in love with life, learning to let go, and finding beauty in the darkest places. Recurring symbols and shifting identities quietly reveal unexpected connections between the pieces.

Throughout the book, six immersive QR experiences continue the story beyond the printed page. They aren't bonus content. They're part of the journey.
