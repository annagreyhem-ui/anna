Letting Go

The darkness wrapped her arms around me and sang me a lullaby.

I faded
and tried to remember all the people I had been.

Sometimes I wished I was Alice.

The water rose higher and higher, up to my lips. Between kisses, I breathed it in. It filled my lungs.

Deep in the glacier crack I saw me, frozen.

I was Alice this morning and I haven’t changed since.

I am Alice.

I wasn’t supposed to die. Was I? Why did I die?

Falling Into the Rabbit Hole

Alice ran after the rabbit. She couldn’t let the little thing get away. She still had so many carrots to give it. So many cuddle sessions already planned. So, Alice ran as fast as she could.

And then — oops. Down she tumbled, right after it. At first the hole was dark and she was afraid, but then she saw that it wasn’t dark at all. It glittered. Streaks of color stretched across the walls like northern lights. The rabbit and she drifted together on a rainbow. Flying felt wonderful. The rabbit drummed its foot without pause, waiting for something. After a while, it grew darker again. They seemed to be reaching the other end of the tunnel.

Alice and the rabbit drifted together on a rainbow. The rabbit stood over her. With one last crack it broke her neck and held her beneath its paws as it tore Alice apart.

Blood Moon

The moon had always been curious. Humans made wishes when stars died. They looked up at the burning, the falling, the fading. Then they closed their eyes and made a wish.

So, the moon tried the other way around, each time a human life flickered out.

She followed Anna. Her becoming, longing, falling, fading. Then the moon closed her eyes.

And when she opened them again, far away in the cold water she saw Alice.

I was alive this morning,
but I have changed a few times since then.

13-13-13

He tastes like salt water and smells like Christmas with a hint of yesterday’s weed.

His black eyes never blink.

I always wanted a pretty death. Maybe a bit tragic and younger than expected.

He was all of that.

I felt so sure that he was the one.

When I saw his eyes, when I heard his silence, I knew.

I fell for him.

He led me home, drowned me.

He was in me while I died.

The End

Stop staring at me.

With those big eyes waiting for a story.

Why do you expect more to happen?

This is the end. You chose the end.

You would have died one day eventually. Why wait 60 more years?

Close the book. Your story has ended here.
